    WELCOME TO SAAD HEADQUARTERS

    Your company in six steps.

    1. Explore the tower. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and right-drag to pan. Try Separate floors, lighting, or Auto orbit.
    2. Enter a department. Choose a floor in the directory or click its label. Every resident has a personal office.
    3. Meet a specialist. Click a person or use the people search. Focus office brings the camera to their desk.
    4. Watch their computer. Click a monitor or Open workstation. See actual commands, tool events, and public replies as they arrive.
    5. Send work. Choose Assign a task, select a project, access mode and time budget, and describe the deliverable.
    6. Review and remember. Open the result, approve or request changes, then save approved knowledge in Workflows & memory.

    Cyan means running, blue means queued, amber means review, and red means an error or interrupted run. Unassigned specialists are on call. Avatar movement and assistant orbs are decoration.
